- **Must communicate** is the job of the section. Design may change how it looks, never this.

- Live copy is kept word for word. Only spelling, grammar and capitalization are fixed, and headings are in sentence case.
- Markers: `[DUMMY: ...]` is missing data. `[DESIGN-DEPENDENT: ...]` is content that depends on an open design choice. `[CONTENT-LEAD: tighten]` is live copy with filler the Content lead may trim. `[UPDATE: ...]` is a fact that will change.

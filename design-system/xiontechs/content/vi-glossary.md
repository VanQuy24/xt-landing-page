# Vietnamese glossary

The fixed Vietnamese term for each key concept on the site. `src/i18n/locales/vi.json` and every file in `content/vi/` use these terms and nothing else. Change a term here first, then everywhere it appears.

Decided 2026-10-12.

## Voice

| Rule                      | Vietnamese           | Note                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The reader                | bạn                  | Always. Never "quý khách", "anh/chị" or "các bạn".                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| The company               | chúng tôi            | "XionTechs" only where the name itself matters, such as a question in the FAQ or a page title.                                                                                                                                                    |
| A question to the company | XionTechs … ?        | The FAQ asks "XionTechs bảo vệ dữ liệu ra sao?", not "Các bạn …?", because "các bạn" would be the company.                                                                                                                                        |
| Sentences                 | Short, subject first | One idea per sentence. No "được" passive when an actor exists, no "việc" + verb as a noun, no "một cách" + adjective, no long "của" chains.                                                                                                       |
| Headings and titles       | Catchy, still true   | This is a landing page, so headings, service names and buttons may sell: "Giải pháp …", "may đo", "Liên hệ ngay", a question as a heading. They never add a claim the English copy doesn't make, and never use the words in "Words we don't use". |

## Words we don't use

Marketing clichés: giải pháp toàn diện, hàng đầu, tiên phong, đồng hành cùng, nâng tầm, kiến tạo, vượt trội, bứt phá, mang đến trải nghiệm, không ngừng nỗ lực.

Translationese: "được" passive where someone does the action, "việc" + verb as a noun ("việc sử dụng"), "một cách" + adjective, chains of three or more "của".

Exception: `a11y.ogAlt` describes the social card image word for word, and the image still shows the old headline. It changes when the image is redrawn.
